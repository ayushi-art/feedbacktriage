Problem

Support teams read hundreds of tickets, but product teams rarely see them in a form they can act on. Without a ranking, the loudest complaint wins instead of the most important one.

Users

Product managers and support leads who need a quick view of customer pain before planning a sprint.

Goal

Go from a batch of raw tickets to a ranked list of fixes in minutes instead of hours.

Success metrics

  • Share of tickets tagged correctly
  • Time from ticket batch to ranked list

Approach

  • Match each ticket to a theme with keyword rules
  • Score tone with a small word list
  • Rank themes with RICE, adjustable by the PM

Trade-offs and limits

Keyword rules are fast and easy to explain, but they miss sarcasm and new wording. The PM stays in control: every impact, confidence and effort value can be changed, and the PM should read sample tickets before acting.

Next steps

  • Replace keyword rules with a trained text classifier
  • Let the PM correct wrong tags and learn from the corrections
  • Track themes week by week to see whether fixes work
